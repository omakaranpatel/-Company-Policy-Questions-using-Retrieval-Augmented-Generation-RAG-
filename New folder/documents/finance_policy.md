# Finance Policy

**Document ID:** FIN-GEN-2024-v1  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Chief Financial Officer

## 1. Overview

This document governs financial controls, purchasing, budgets, and reimbursement compliance for all departments.

## 2. Budget and Spending Authority

- Department budgets are allocated quarterly
- Unbudgeted expenses over **$1,000** require budget owner approval
- Capital expenditures over **$10,000** follow the CapEx approval workflow (Finance form FC-101)

## 3. Corporate Card Program

- Issued to employees with recurring business expenses or travel frequency above 4 trips/year
- Personal use is prohibited; violations may result in card revocation and disciplinary action
- Reconcile corporate card transactions within **5 business days** of statement close

## 4. Reimbursement Compliance

Finance audits a random **10% sample** of reimbursements each quarter. Common audit failures include:

- Missing or illegible receipts
- Expenses without business justification
- Duplicate submissions
- Incorrect cost center coding

Repeated violations may require pre-approval for all future expenses.

## 5. Tax and Documentation

- Gifts to employees over **$25** must be reported for tax purposes
- Contractor payments require W-9 or equivalent before first disbursement
- International reimbursements may require additional tax documentation

## 6. Fraud and Misrepresentation

Knowingly submitting false expense claims is grounds for termination and potential legal action. Report suspected fraud anonymously via the Ethics Hotline: **1-888-555-0147**.

## 7. Payment Methods

Reimbursements are issued via ACH to the bank account on file in the HRIS system. Update banking details at least **5 business days** before the payment run date.

## 8. Currency and Exchange Rates

- Foreign expenses converted using the OANDA rate on the transaction date
- Conversion fees are reimbursable up to **3%** when documented on the receipt

## 9. Contact

Finance shared services: **finance-help@company.com**
