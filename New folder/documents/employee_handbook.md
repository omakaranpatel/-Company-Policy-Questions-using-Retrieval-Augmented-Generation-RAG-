# Employee Handbook — Reimbursement & Benefits Excerpt

**Document ID:** HR-HDB-2024-v5  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources

## 1. Welcome

This excerpt covers reimbursement-related benefits and employee responsibilities. Refer to department policies for detailed rules.

## 2. Wellness Stipend

Full-time employees receive an annual **$600 wellness stipend** for gym memberships, fitness apps, or mental health apps. This is **not** submitted through standard expense reports:

- Enroll via the Benefits Portal by March 31 each year
- Reimbursement is taxable and paid once annually in April
- Unused stipend does not roll over

## 3. Remote Work Equipment

Remote employees may claim a one-time **$500 home office setup allowance** in their first 90 days of remote work. Eligible items:

- Monitor, keyboard, mouse, desk chair
- Desk or ergonomic accessories

Ongoing internet reimbursement: up to **$50/month** with proof of service and manager attestation of business need.

## 4. Professional Development

Each employee has a **$2,000 annual learning budget** for courses, conferences, and certifications related to their role. Conference travel follows the Travel Policy. Registration must be pre-approved by manager.

## 5. Employee Responsibilities

Employees must:

- Act in the company's best financial interest
- Retain receipts for 7 years per IRS guidelines (digital copies accepted)
- Complete annual compliance training on expense ethics (due every December)

## 6. New Hire Grace Period

New hires have a **60-day grace period** for their first expense report submission while onboarding to systems.

## 7. Leave and Expenses

Expenses incurred during approved leave (e.g., conference while on PTO) are eligible if pre-approved. Expenses purely personal during leave are not reimbursable.

## 8. HR Support

HR Business Partners can clarify policy intent but cannot override Finance approval limits. Contact **hr-help@company.com**.
