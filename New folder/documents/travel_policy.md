# Travel Policy

**Document ID:** HR-TRV-2024-v2  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources & Finance

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all employees traveling on company business, including domestic and international trips.

## 2. Pre-Trip Approval

- All travel must be approved in the travel management system **before booking**
- Trips estimated to cost over **$2,500** require Department Head approval
- International travel requires **HR and Security clearance** at least 10 business days in advance

## 3. Booking Guidelines

### Air Travel

- Book economy class for flights under 6 hours
- Premium economy or business class requires VP approval for flights over 6 hours
- Use approved corporate travel vendors; personal bookings require reimbursement justification

### Lodging

- Nightly hotel cap: **$200** (domestic), **$350** (international tier-1 cities)
- Extended stays over 14 nights require a furnished apartment or corporate housing when available

### Ground Transportation

- Use ride-share or rental cars when public transit is impractical
- Rental car daily cap: **$75** plus insurance
- Personal vehicle mileage reimbursed at **$0.67 per mile** (IRS standard rate)

## 4. Travel Per Diem

Employees may choose actual expenses or per diem (not both for the same day):

| Location Type | Daily Per Diem |
|---------------|----------------|
| Domestic – standard | $65 |
| Domestic – high-cost (NYC, SF, DC) | $85 |
| International | $95 – $150 (by country table in appendix) |

## 5. Travel Advances

- Advances up to **$1,000** available upon manager approval
- Must reconcile within **15 days** of trip completion
- Unreconciled advances may be deducted from payroll

## 6. Safety and Compliance

- Register international trips with Global Security
- Follow destination health advisories; vaccinations may be company-paid when required for work
- Do not travel to countries under active government travel advisories Level 4 without CEO approval
