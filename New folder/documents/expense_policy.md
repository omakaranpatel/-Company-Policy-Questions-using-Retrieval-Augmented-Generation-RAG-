# Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** FIN-EXP-2024-v3  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Finance Department

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how employees submit, approve, and receive reimbursement for business-related expenses.

## 2. Eligible Expenses

Employees may claim reimbursement for expenses incurred solely for legitimate business purposes, including:

- Client meals and entertainment (with prior manager approval for amounts over $150)
- Office supplies and equipment under $500 per item
- Professional development courses and certifications
- Mobile phone bills (business use portion only, up to 50% of total bill)
- Software subscriptions required for job duties

## 3. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are **not** eligible for reimbursement:

- Personal travel, meals, or entertainment
- Alcohol unless directly tied to an approved client event
- Traffic fines, parking tickets, or legal penalties
- Gym memberships or wellness programs (see Employee Handbook for wellness stipend)
- Gifts to clients exceeding $75 per recipient per year without VP approval

## 4. Submission Requirements

- Submit expense reports within **30 days** of incurring the expense
- Expenses older than **90 days** will not be reimbursed without CFO exception
- All receipts must be itemized; credit card statements alone are insufficient
- Receipts over $25 are mandatory; under $25 requires a written explanation

## 5. Approval Workflow

| Amount | Approver |
|--------|----------|
| Up to $500 | Direct Manager |
| $501 – $5,000 | Department Head |
| Over $5,000 | CFO |

## 6. Per Diem Rates (Domestic)

When meals are not itemized on receipts, use these daily limits:

- Breakfast: $18
- Lunch: $22
- Dinner: $35
- Incidentals: $5

## 7. Payment Timeline

Approved reimbursements are processed on the **15th and last business day** of each month via direct deposit.

## 8. Contact

For expense policy questions, email **expenses@company.com** or contact the Finance Help Desk at extension 4500.
